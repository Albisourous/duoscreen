# DuoScreen Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 21, 2026

DuoScreen collects no data.

- No personal information is collected, stored, or transmitted.
- No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs are included.
- No accounts, sign-in, or in-app purchases.
- Browsing happens entirely on your device in two `WKWebView` panes. Websites you visit are subject to their own privacy policies.
- The only on-device storage is your pane-split preference, saved via `UserDefaults`. It never leaves the device.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL before the change ships.

**Contact:** open an issue at https://github.com/Albisourous/duoscreen/issues
